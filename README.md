## 👋 About Me

I’m a **Data Analyst and Business Intelligence professional** focused on transforming complex data into clear, actionable business insights.

I work across the analytics lifecycle — from **data extraction and transformation to analysis, visualization, KPI development and business reporting**.

My core toolkit includes **Power BI, SQL, Python, Tableau, Advanced Excel, Power Query, DAX, ETL and data modeling**, with hands-on experience across modern cloud and data platforms.

### What I do

- 📊 Build interactive **Power BI & Tableau dashboards**
- 🧮 Develop analytical solutions using **SQL, Python & DAX**
- 🔄 Design and automate **ETL / data transformation workflows**
- 🧱 Build analytical **data models and KPI frameworks**
- 🔍 Convert raw data into meaningful **business insights**
- ⚙️ Work with cloud and modern data platforms
- 🤝 Translate business requirements into practical analytical solutions
